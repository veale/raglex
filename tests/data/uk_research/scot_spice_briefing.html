The Scottish Parliament and the Ethical Standards Commissioner

Author(s): Courtney Aitken

The Ethical Standards Commissioner is an independent regulator appointed by the Scottish Parliamentary Corporate Body, approved by MSPs and underpinned by legislation. This briefing explains the role of the Ethical Standards Commissioner, its functions and its relationship with the Scottish Parliament.
